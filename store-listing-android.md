## Full description
Challenge FruityFly, the clever little fruit fly, to a game of chess. Whether you are learning the moves or sharpening your tactics, Chess vs FruityFly makes every game fun.

Play your way:
- Vs FruityFly: an adaptive opponent that adjusts to your skill, with hints whenever you want them
- Two players: pass and play on one device
- Online: create a room, share the code, and play friends anywhere
- Friends: add friends with a friend code, see when they are online, and challenge them to a game

Track your progress with the player dashboard: wins, losses, a skill tier from Basic to Advanced, and detailed post-game analysis that shows your best moves and where you slipped. Unfinished games save automatically, and you can resume right where you left off.

No account needed to play. Sign in to unlock post-game analysis, your progress dashboard, and friend challenges.
