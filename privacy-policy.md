# Privacy Policy for Chess vs FruityFly

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Chess vs FruityFly ("the app") is a chess game you can play against an adaptive
computer opponent, with a friend on the same device, or online with other players.
This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

**Account information (only if you sign in).** Playing does not require an account.
If you choose to sign in with email or Google, we store your email address and a
unique user ID through Firebase Authentication. We never show your email address
to other players.

**Gameplay data.** When you play online or while signed in, we store your moves,
game results, move counts, skill tier, and post-game analysis through Firebase
Realtime Database. This powers online rooms, your progress dashboard, and game
history.

**Friends and presence.** If you use the friends feature, we store your friend
code, your friends list, and whether you are currently online or in a game so
friends can see when you are available. Player names shown in the app are
auto-generated and contain no personal information.

**Analytics.** The app uses Google Analytics to understand how it is used, for
example which game modes are played and how many games are completed. Analytics
data is aggregated and contains no name, email, or contact information.

**Device data.** Sign-in sessions are kept on your own device so you stay signed
in between visits.

## How we use information

We use the information only to run the game: signing you in, syncing online
games, showing your progress, and improving the app. We do not sell, rent, or
share your personal information with advertisers or any third party.

## Third-party services

The app uses these services, each with its own privacy policy:

- Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC)
- Google Analytics (Google LLC)

## Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data and friend data are kept while your account exists. If you want
your account and its data deleted, contact us at the address below and we will
delete it.

## Children

The app is a general-audience board game and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. Accounts are only created when someone
chooses to sign in.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at
https://chessfruitfly.com/privacy-policy.html with a new last-updated date.
